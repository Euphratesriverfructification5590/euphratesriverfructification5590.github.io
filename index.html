🎥 How to Use Suflyor Like a Pro

Here's a quick workflow that works for content creators, educators, and live streamers:

  • Write or paste your script into the app's text editor. You can also import a text file.
  • Start a floating session: Tap the "Float" button — Suflyor will create a small movable window on top of other apps.
  • Open your camera, Instagram, or TikTok — the script window stays visible.
  • Begin speaking. The text scrolls with your pace. You can pause by stopping your speech, or use the on-screen controls.
  • Adjust settings: Change font size, background opacity, and scroll sensitivity to match your comfort.

🔧 Troubleshooting & FAQs

App won't install?

Make sure you've allowed "Install from unknown sources" (the app's download page has a step-by-step guide for your specific phone model). Also check that you have enough storage space.

Voice scrolling is too fast/slow?

Open the settings panel (gear icon) and adjust the "Scroll speed" slider. The app learns your pace over time, but you can fine-tune it anytime.

Does this work on tablets?

Yes! Suflyor runs on Android tablets. For the best experience, use a tablet stand and connect a Bluetooth microphone.

Can I use it with other languages?

Currently, the voice recognition supports English and Russian. You can still use the manual scroll feature for any language.
